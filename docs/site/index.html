Part of your development conversation

A graph that follows
your project.

From the first sketch to your next change, keep a view of what the program is meant to do and what it currently does.

  1. 01

    Agree on the flow

    Describe your project. See its first graph in the conversation, clarify the details that affect the flow, and adjust it until it matches your intent. Open the linked Web view for a closer look.

  2. 02

    Develop and inspect

    Continue coding with your preferred tools. At the end of each iteration, explore the updated graph, open a function, or add a note beside something you want to revisit.

  3. 03

    Review the result

    Check how well the graph covers the implementation and how the program behaves in the agreed test cases. Follow a reported issue back to the affected flow and review it after a fix.
